## What makes GenX different

- **GenX's own Trier codecs.** **Trier MPEG Video** decodes MPEG-2 video, and **Trier MPEG** decodes MPEG audio Layers I and II. On compatible Android devices, they let GenX play channels whose picture or sound needs a decoder the device doesn't provide.
- **Pass It Along.** Share GenX from one Android phone to another, send a download link, or share the app over local Wi-Fi with a QR code or six-digit code. [Pass It Along guide](docs/share-links.md)
- **Install on TV from your phone.** Pick a compatible Google TV or Android TV, enter the code shown on the TV, and approve the install with the remote. [Install on TV guide](docs/send-to-tv.md)
- **Build your own channel lineup.** Scan public sources you choose, check streams before saving them, and add your own playlists or Paid TV accounts.

## New in 0.1.33

- **Share Free TV favorites.** Send your favorites to another device on the same network, or export and import them as a file.
- **My Teams.** Follow your teams, look up verified game listings and watch links, and see Paid TV replay dates when available.

## What you can do

- **Watch live TV.** Browse Free TV or sign in with your own Xtream provider in Paid TV. Find channels with favorites, history, search, and voice search where supported.
- **See what's on.** Browse the program guide for current and upcoming shows, with channel and program search plus sports filters.
- **Explore GenXtra.** Watch and download public-domain films and classic TV.
- **Watch your way.** Go fullscreen, use captions where available, and cast to compatible Chromecast or DLNA screens. On supported live streams, see how much is buffered and set a buffer target for that stream.
- **Keep your library on your device.** Channel lists, favorites, and watch history are stored locally. GenX Streamer uses no analytics or tracking.
