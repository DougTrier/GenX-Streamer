About the GenX Streamer app

What is GenX Streamer?

GenX Streamer is a free, open-source TV player by Doug Trier. Watch public live channels, browse a program guide and explore GenXtra's public-domain films and classic TV. You can also bring your own playlists or provider account.

Which devices does it support?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), and Android 7.0 or newer on ARM devices, including Android TV, Google TV, Amazon Fire TV and Fire TV Stick running Android-based Fire OS, phones, tablets and handhelds. There is currently no iPhone, iPad or Mac version.

Which Android APK should I use on Fire TV?

Fire TV and Fire TV Stick models that run Android-based Fire OS use the same ARM Android APK. See the Fire TV installation guide.

Does it include a paid TV subscription?

No. The app is free; paid services require your own provider account. Channel availability depends on the source and your location. A channel or guide listing does not guarantee that a stream is available or will play.

How do I install it?

Download the official Windows ZIP or Android APK, then follow the installation and setup guide. You can also install on a Google TV or Android TV from an Android phone using Pass It Along.

Content sources and rights

GenX Streamer starts with an empty channel lineup and includes no subscriptions. It searches publicly available sources it can reach, checks which streams respond, and lets you choose what to save. You can also add your own playlists or provider accounts.

Publishers and hosts control what they make publicly available. Users control the sources they add to their own devices. GenX is responsible for how the app handles those sources and protects user data.

Source availability changes over time. Discovery results reflect what GenX can reach when you search. A stream responding shows that it was reachable at the time of the check.

For requests to remove or restrict access to the underlying content, contact the publisher or hosting service directly.

GenX Streamer is free and open source (Apache 2.0) by Doug Trier. If it earns a place on your screen, you can support it on GitHub Sponsors.
